## 2. Verified correct in v2 and kept
- Coverage: 7 complete months; all 17 tables present.
- Dedup key is `payment_id`, not `payment_reference` (a reference-based dedup would delete ₹14.7 Cr of distinct payments).
- Timezone conversion, agent SCD-2, borrower survivorship, cash-verified PTPs, fixed-cohort panel.
- The 11% reconstruction (+10.99%), the calendar decomposition, the trend tests and the channel-vs-baseline test.
- The counterfactual and its "not causally identified" verdict.
- Evidence-bounded investment logic and the experiment power analysis.
