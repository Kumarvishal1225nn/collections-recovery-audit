# Tableau handoff (datasets, relationships, KPIs, recommended views)

No Tableau work has been done. This document and `outputs/tableau/` are everything the dashboard phase needs. All files are regenerated by `run_pipeline.py` (and the last four by the notebook), and `tests/test_golden.py` checks that their totals reconcile to the golden dataset.

**Conventions:** money in ₹ (divide by 1e7 for crore); timestamps in IST; `month_start` is the first day of a calendar month; August 2026 has only 8 days (`is_complete = false`) and must be filtered out of any month comparison. No personal data (names, phones, emails) is included.

## 2. Relationships (Tableau data model)

```
accounts (account_id)  1 ──── * account_month (account_id, month_start)
accounts (account_id)  1 ──── * payments (account_id)
kpi_monthly (month_start) 1 ── * account_month / payments / channel_month (month_start)
```

- Use **relationships**, not joins, between `accounts`, `account_month` and `payments`. Joining `payments` to `account_month` on `account_id` alone would repeat each payment eight times.
- `kpi_monthly`, `kpi_daily`, `segment_month`, `campaign_summary`, `funnel_monthly` and the investment tables are **pre-aggregated**. Use each as its own data source; never join them to row-level tables.
- `collected` in `account_month` and `net_amount` in `payments` are the same money at two grains. Sum one or the other, never both.

## 3. KPI definitions to use (full rationale in `docs/metric_definitions.md`)

| KPI | Tableau calculation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Recovery per day | `SUM([net_collections]) / SUM([days_in_month])` | `kpi_monthly` |
| Recovery rate (fixed book) | `[recovery_rate_pct]` | `kpi_monthly` |
| Paying-account rate | `SUM(INT([paid_flag])) / COUNTD([account_id])` per month | `account_month` |
| Reported MoM | `[mom_success_with_dups_pct]` | `reported_vs_audited_mom` |
| Audited MoM | table calc on recovery per day | `kpi_monthly` |
| Contact rate | `[contact_rate_pct]` (answered only) | `kpi_monthly` |
| PTP kept, label vs cash | `[ptp_kept_reported_pct]`, `[ptp_kept_verified_pct]` | `kpi_monthly` |
| Channel lift | `[pay_within_7d_pct] - [baseline_pay_within_7d_pct]` | `channel_conversion` |

**Rules:** never show raw month totals as growth; always filter `is_complete = true`; show a confidence interval or the words "not significant" next to any change; never rank agents.

## 4. Recommended views (one screen, in reading order)

| # | View | Chart | Data | Business question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verdict banner | text + 4 number tiles (reported +11% vs audited +0.3%; run-rate ₹/month; PTP kept 4% vs 50%; channel lift ≈0) | `kpi_monthly`, `channel_conversion` | Is the 11% real? |
| 2 | Reported vs audited | bars of recovery per day by month, labelled with both MoM figures | `kpi_monthly`, `reported_vs_audited_mom` | What actually happened? |
| 3 | Where the 11% comes from | waterfall: reported → duplicates → calendar days → audited | `mom_by_definition` | Why is the number wrong? |
| 4 | Definitions that mislead | two-line small multiples: PTP kept (label vs cash), contact rate (with vs without voicemail), recovery rate (status-based vs fixed book) | `kpi_monthly` | Which KPIs can't be trusted? |
| 5 | Does any channel work? | horizontal bars with a baseline reference line | `channel_conversion` | Where does contact add value? |
| 6 | ₹10 Cr options | table or bullet chart: incremental recovery (point and range) against cost, with verdict | `investment_decision_table` | Where should the money go? |
| 7 | Data-quality panel | table of the five largest issues with rows and ₹ affected | `data_quality_rules`, `duplicate_payment_classes` | How reliable is this? |

A drill-down sheet can use `segment_month` (filter by `dim`) to show that no DPD, product, state or risk segment behaves differently.
